Quick answers

Before you join

Does joining guarantee a match?

No. This is an early-interest pilot. Matches depend on availability, compatibility and mutual consent.

Will you share my phone number?

Not publicly and not without your explicit permission. Only share details through a private channel you trust.

Is there a fee right now?

No payment is being collected through this website. Any future fee should be explained clearly before you agree.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. You can withdraw your interest and request deletion of information you shared with the operator.
